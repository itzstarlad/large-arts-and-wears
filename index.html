Helpful information

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about custom gifts, printing, branding and our creative services.

1. What products and services do you offer?

We offer logo design, customised gifts, throw pillows, vests, phone pouches, jotters, acrylic key holders and clocks, surprise packages, photo frames, paper bags, birthday cards, branded nylon, house painting, wallpaper installation and artwork.

2. How do I place an order?

Choose a service on this website and tap its WhatsApp order button, use the order form, or contact us directly on WhatsApp with your requirements.

3. How much do your products and services cost?

Prices depend on the item, design, materials and quantity. Contact us on WhatsApp for a current quote and any available discounts.

4. Can I customise a product with my own picture or design?

Yes. Tell us what you have in mind and share your preferred pictures, text, logo or design inspiration when you enquire.

5. How long will my order take?

Production time depends on the service, design and order quantity. We will confirm the estimated completion time before you place your order.

6. Do you accept bulk and corporate orders?

Yes, you can enquire about bulk orders for events, businesses, schools, organisations and celebrations. Share the quantity and deadline for a quotation.

7. Do you deliver to Ibadan and other locations?

Please contact us to confirm delivery availability to Ibadan or your location, the delivery fee and the expected delivery time.

8. Can I pick up my order myself?

Pickup can be discussed when your order is ready. Please contact us first to confirm the collection location and a convenient time.

9. What payment methods do you accept?

Ask us on WhatsApp for the currently available payment options and payment instructions before making a transfer or payment.

10. Can you help me create a logo or artwork if I have no design?

Yes. Explain your idea, preferred colours, wording and style. We can discuss a suitable design direction with you.

11. Can I see a design preview before production?

Preview arrangements depend on the type of job. Please request a preview when discussing your order and confirm any applicable design terms.

12. Where are your offices located?

Head Office: Momak 1 Street, Oke-Idawo Area, Iseyin, Oyo State. Brand Office: Opposite Afoo Filling Station, Isalu Area, Iseyin, Oyo State.

13. Can I request an urgent order?

Let us know your deadline and the service you need. We will check our schedule and tell you whether we can meet it.

14. What information should I provide when ordering?

Share the product or service, quantity, preferred size, colours, text or images, delivery or pickup preference and your required date.
